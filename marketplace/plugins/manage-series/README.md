# Manage Series

Puts the chosen books in one series and numbers them. After calibre's [Manage Series](https://github.com/kiwidude68/calibre_plugins/tree/main/manage_series) plugin by Grant Drake ([`dialogs.py`](https://github.com/kiwidude68/calibre_plugins/tree/main/manage_series/dialogs.py), [`book.py`](https://github.com/kiwidude68/calibre_plugins/tree/main/manage_series/book.py)), GPL-3.0.

Choose books, then **Set series…**: the series (the first book's by default), the number to start at, and the order: as chosen, original series index, original series name, published date or title. **Remove from series** clears the series.

Not ported: indents (.1 and .01 steps), locked indexes and empty placeholder books, which need the plugin's table.
